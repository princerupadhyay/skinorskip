Why These 3 Myths Won't Die

"Sabne bola hai yeh kaam karta hai" — yeh sabse bada reason hai jo in myths ko zinda rakhta hai, kyunki koi bhi inhe actually question nahi karta. Lekin honestly compare karo har ek ko real mechanism se:

  1. Ice cube: Numbing aur mild swelling-reduction de sakta hai temporarily, lekin pimple ka underlying cause — clogged pore aur bacteria — usse touch tak nahi hota. Zyada der ice rakhna broken skin ko aur irritate kar sakta hai.
  2. Toothpaste: Skin ke liye formulate hi nahi kiya gaya — menthol aur baking soda jaise ingredients surface ko dry ya burn kar sakte hain, aur inflammation ko address nahi karte. Koi bhi improvement coincidence hai, treatment nahi.
  3. Dry razor se shave karna: Active pimple ke upar se razor chalana bacteria spread karta hai aur cut lagne ka risk badhata hai — result: ek pimple se poori area mein breakout.

Teeno myths mein common problem yeh hai ki yeh sirf surface-level ya temporary relief dete hain, asli cause — clogged pores aur bacteria — ko touch nahi karte.

Frequently asked questions

Salicylic acid pimple pe kitni der mein kaam karta hai?

Ek single pimple pe 2-3 din mein visible reduction dikhna shuru ho sakta hai, lekin overall breakout pattern improve hone mein 4-6 hafte ka consistent use lagta hai. Yeh overnight fix nahi, ek gradual, real improvement hai.

Kya pain ke liye ice use kar sakte hain, pimple clear karne ke liye nahi?

Haan — agar ek pimple bahut painful ya swollen hai, ice thodi der ke liye numbing aur mild swelling-reduction de sakta hai. Bas isse "cure" mat samjho, sirf temporary comfort ke liye use karo, aur direct skin pe zyada der mat rakho.

Active acne ke saath shave karna safe hai?

Directly pimple ke upar se razor chalana avoid karo — bacteria spread hone aur cut lagne dono ka risk hai. Baaki area shave kar sakte ho, bas active spots ke around gently kaam karo, aur razor clean rakho.

Toothpaste se pimple sach mein sukh jaata hai?

Kuch cases mein baking soda aur menthol ki wajah se surface pe temporary drying effect dikh sakta hai, lekin yeh underlying inflammation ko address nahi karta aur skin ko irritate ya burn kar sakta hai. Yeh coincidence hai, real fix nahi.
