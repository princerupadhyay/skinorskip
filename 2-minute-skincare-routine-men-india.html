Step 1: Nivea Men Oil Control Face Wash (100g) — ₹199–235

Subah aur raat, dono time face ko gently wet karo aur is face wash se 30 seconds tak massage karo and phir wash off. Ye oil aur excess shine control karta hai bina skin ko bahut zyada dry kiye — joki monsoon mein zyadatar facewash karte hain. Bar soap se ye kaafi behtar hai kyunki soap face ki skin ke liye kafi harsh hota hai (no matter aap ladka ho ya ladki) aur wo zyada oil production trigger kar sakta hai.

Ek face wash jo genuinely oily skin ke liye bana hai, ₹200 ke aas-paas aur isse behtar cost-per-use is routine mein kuch nahi milega.

Does skipping moisturizer actually hurt oily skin?

Ye bahut he common doubt hai jab log dekhte hain ki is routine mein separate moisturizer nahi hai. Sach ye hai ki "oily skin ko moisturizer ki zaroorat nahi" wali baat aadhi galat hai. Skin ko hydration chahiye hoti hai, warna wo zyada oil produce karke compensate karne lagti hai. Lekin is specific 2-product version mein, matte sunscreen already ek lightweight base provide kar deta hai jo zyadatar oily, monsoon-affected skin ke liye kaafi hoti hai. Ye poora ek alag heavy moisturizer step ko add karne se better hai jo humidity mein aur bhi zyada oily feel karwa sakta hai.

Agar skin genuinely tight ya patchy feel ho raha ho kisi din, ek lightweight gel moisturizer add karna theek hai, lekin har roz ke liye ye 2 products he more than enough hain.

Frequently asked questions

Do guys really need sunscreen in India?

Haan bhai, bilkul — chahe skin oily ho ya dry, India mein sun har roz tan aur dark spots ka risk badhata hai, aur ek matte-finish sunscreen ye without kisi greasy feel ke handle kar sakta hai.

Can I skip face wash and just use soap?

Technically chal jaayega, par body soap face ki skin ke liye kaafi harsh hota hai kyuki wo skin ki natural oil ko strip kar sakta hai, jo ultimately zyada oil production trigger karta hai. So ek proper oil-control face wash is better than soap.

How long until oily skin improves with this routine?

Zyadatar log 2-3 hafton ke consistent use ke baad shine aur breakouts mein noticeable farak dekhte hain, lekin ye har skin ke type pe depend karta hai. Ye koi quick-fix nahi hai, ek daily habit hai, jo consistent use he farak dikhayegi.

Do I need a separate moisturizer even with oily skin?

Is 2-product version mein nahi because ek lightweight, matte sunscreen already kaafi hydration-jaisa base de deta hai roz ke liye. But haan agar skin bahut zyada dry-patchy feel ho toh alag baat hai, then you can go for separate moisturizer.
