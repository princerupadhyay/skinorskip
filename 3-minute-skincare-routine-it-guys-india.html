Why "No Time" Isn't a Valid Excuse Before a Standup Either

"Mere paas time nahi hai, seedha laptop khol ke call join karna hai" — yeh sabse common excuse hai jo desk-job guys skincare skip karne ke liye dete hain. Lekin honestly compare karo: tumhara current "no-routine" bhi actually time leta hai — bas alag jagah pe. Mid-call shine ko camera angle badal ke chhupana, ya AC dryness se hone wali irritation ko ignore karna — yeh sab bhi minutes hain jo waste ho rahe hain, bas ek proper routine ki tarah organized nahi hain.

Ek chhoti comparison dekh lo:

  1. No routine, seedha call join karna: Overnight oil aur AC dryness dono ek saath skin pe rehte hain — result: call ke beech mein hi shine notice hone lagta hai.
  2. No routine, bas tissue se wipe karna: Temporary fix hai, lekin oil production actually badh jaata hai baar-baar wipe karne se skin ko dry karke — result: agli call tak phir wahi problem.
  3. 2-product, 3-minute routine: Ek fixed morning habit, predictable result — kam shine, aur AC dryness bhi under control poore din.

Third option mein sabse kam total time jaata hai jab mid-call camera-adjusting aur tissue-wiping minutes ko bhi count karo.

Myth-Bust: Oily Skin Still Needs a Moisturizer

"Oily skin ko moisturizer ki zaroorat nahi" wala tag desk-job guys ke beech unfairly popular hai kyunki logically lagta hai extra oil pe extra product kyun daalna. Reality opposite hai — jab skin ko external moisturizer nahi milta, especially AC dryness ke saath, woh apna khud ka oil production badha ke compensate karti hai. Result: exactly wahi shine jo tum avoid karna chahte ho.

2 steps, ek fixed morning habit, 3 minute — yeh utna hi maintainable hai jitna roz laptop on karna. Isse "high-maintenance" bolna galat hai; yeh toh actually sabse simple tarika hai video-call shine ko root cause se fix karne ka bina kuch bhi compromise kiye.

Frequently asked questions

Video calls pe hi zyada shiny kyun dikhta hoon?

Screen light aur ring light jaisa effect skin ke oil ko highlight karte hain jo naked eye mein utna notice nahi hota. AC office ki dryness bhi paradoxically zyada oil production trigger karti hai — skin apna moisture loss compensate karne ki koshish karti hai.

WFH aur office days ke liye same routine chalega?

Bilkul — routine location-specific nahi hai, screen-time aur AC-exposure specific hai. Chahe ghar se kaam karo ya office se, morning face wash aur moisturizer wahi rehta hai.

Sirf face wash kaafi hai ya toner bhi chahiye?

Beginners ke liye toner zaroori step nahi hai — face wash aur moisturizer ka basic combo already oil aur dryness dono handle kar leta hai. Toner add-on hai, foundation nahi.

Oily skin hone ke bawajood moisturizer lagana zaroori hai?

Haan, aur yeh sabse common mistake hai jo pehli-baar routine follow kar rahe guys karte hain. Moisturizer skip karna oil production ko aur trigger karta hai — result: video call pe aur zyada shine.
