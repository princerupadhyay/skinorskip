Why Your Regular Routine Stops Working the Moment Monsoon Hits?

Jo moisturizer summer mein light feel hota tha, wahi monsoon ki humidity mein heavy aur sticky kyu lagne lagta hai? It's because sweat aur nami milke pores ko clog kardete hain, aur agar tumhari skin already oily ya combination hai, toh yeh combo directly breakouts mein convert ho jaata hai. Iske upar, hard water aur pollution bhi skin barrier ko weak karte hain, jiski wajah se products jo pehle suit karte the, ab irritate karne lagte hain.

Isliye monsoon mein skincare routine ko simplify karna hi sabse smart move hota hai — kam products, lekin har product ka ek clear purpose hona chahiye. So isliye yahaan hum exactly wahi kar rahe hain: Ek oil-control face wash, Ek serum jo sebum aur barrier dono ko handle kare, aur Ek sunscreen jo greasy na lage aur sath he UV rays se bhi protect kare.

The 3-Product Monsoon Routine (Under ₹650)

Yeh routine specifically oily aur breakout-prone skin ke liye banaya gaya hai, lekin combination skin wale bhi easily follow kar sakte hain. Total cost roughly ₹630–₹650 ke beech baithti hai depending on jahan se order kar rahe ho.

1. Plum Green Tea Pore Cleansing Face Wash (100ml) — ₹199–₹256

Iss face wash mein glycolic acid aur green tea hai, jo excess oil ko remove karta hai bina skin ke natural oil ko strip off kiye matlab bina skin ko over-dry kiye. Monsoon mein subah aur raat dono waqt iss facewash ko use karo. Yeh pores ko clean rakhta hai jiske wajah se sweat aur humidity combine hoke breakouts trigger nahi kar pate hain.

Face wash ko bar soap se replace karna sabse pehla aur sabse zyaada impact karne wala change hai. Sirf ₹200 ke andar hi mil jaata hai aur skin barrier ko strip nahi karta.

2. Minimalist 10% Niacinamide Face Serum (30ml) — approx ₹399

Niacinamide sebum control mein help karta hai aur barrier ko bhi support karta hai, which means yeh serum hi iss routine mein moisturizer ka kaam bhi kar leta hai. Face wash ke baad aur sunscreen se pehle kuch drops lagao iss serrum ke, aur koi extra heavy cream ki zaroorat nahi padegi.

Ek hi serum se moisturizer wali job bhi ho jaati hai. Humid weather mein separate cream lagana skip kar sakte ho, jab tak skin bahut zyaada dry na ho.

3. Minimalist Sunscreen SPF 50 PA++++ with Niacinamide & Multi-Vitamins (50g) — ₹379

Yeh sunscreen no white cast deta hai aur iska finish non-greasy hai, jo monsoon ke sticky-humid feel ke liye perfect hai. Cloudy din ho ya rain, isse kabhi bhi skip mat karna because UV rays clouds ke through bhi kaafi had tak reach karte hain.

Total routine cost approx ₹630–₹650 baithta hai teeno products milake. Sunscreen yahaan par non-negotiable step hai, chahe sky cloudy hi kyun na ho.

Myth-Bust: "Baarish Mein Sunscreen Ki Zaroorat Nahi"

Sabse common and sabse badi galatfehmi hai jo log carry karte hain ki "dhoop nahi hai toh sunscreen kyun lagayein". Reality yeh hai ki clouds UV rays ko fully block nahi karte, aur baarish ke beech bhi UV index kaafi high reh sakta hai. Iske upar, monsoon mein skin already sensitive hoti hai due to humidity and pollution, jisse sun damage ke chances aur bhi zyada badh jate hain.

Doosra reason: Log sunscreen isliye bhi skip karte hain kyunki purane formulas greasy aur white-cast wale hote the. Lekin ab matte-finish, no-white-cast wale options easily market available hain and budget mein bhi hain. Toh apne face par sunscreen lagao, excuse nahi.

Frequently Asked Questions

Kya monsoon mein toner zaroori hai?

Nahi, toner koi compulsory step nahi hai. Agar face wash aur serum sahi kaam kar rahe hain, toh toner ke bina bhi skin theek se manage ho jaati hai. Extra step, extra kharcha, utna zaroori nahi.

Agar meri skin oily hai toh kya moisturizer skip kar sakte hain humidity mein?

Bilkul nahi. Niacinamide serum hi is routine mein moisturizer ka kaam bhi karta hai, isliye alag se heavy cream lagane ki koi zaroorat nahi, lekin barrier ko bina kuch diye chhodna oiliness aur badha sakta hai.

Yeh routine kitne mahine chalega before repurchase?

Face wash aur sunscreen roughly 2 mahine chalte hain daily use mein, serum thoda zyada chalta hai kyunki thodi si quantity hi kaafi hoti hai. So poora set milake 2 se 2.5 mahine ka investment maan sakte hain.

Kya baarish ke din sunscreen zaroori hai jab dhoop hi nahi hai?

Haan, badalon ke peeche se bhi UV rays kaafi had tak penetrate karte hain, aur monsoon mein humidity ki wajah se skin already sensitive hoti hai. Isliye sunscreen skip karna sabse common mistake hai.

Kya yeh routine hostel ya PG mein manage karna easy hai?

Haan, sirf 3 products hain aur poora routine 5 minute se kam mein ho jaata hai. Shared bathroom aur limited counter space ko dhyan mein rakhke hi banaya gaya hai.
