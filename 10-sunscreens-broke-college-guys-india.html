How We Actually Picked These 10

Humne yeh list random nahi banayi. Har product ka SPF rating, live listing price (Amazon, Flipkart, aur Blinkit cross-check karke), aur size-to-price ratio dekha. Jo bhi product sirf "natural" ya "herbal" claim pe bik raha tha bina clear SPF number ke label pe, use hataya — kyunki bina SPF rating ke, tum guess kar rahe ho, protection nahi paa rahe.

Ek honest disclaimer: do products — Plum aur Dermatouch — ke prices abhi estimate hain, kyunki listings pe exact number fluctuate kar raha tha jab humne check kiya. In dono ko order karne se pehle current price zaroor confirm kar lena, baaki 8 verified hain.

SPF50 vs SPF30 — What Actually Matters

Zyada log sochte hain SPF50 automatically SPF30 se "double protection" deta hai — number dekh ke aisa lagta hai. Reality thodi different hai: SPF30 lagbhag 97% UVB rays block karta hai, SPF50 lagbhag 98%. Farak real hai lekin utna dramatic nahi jitna number suggest karta hai.

Jo cheez actually farak daalti hai woh hai reapplication. Agar tum din mein ek baar laga ke bhool jaate ho — chahe SPF30 ho ya SPF50 — protection kam hoti jaati hai sweat aur baar-baar touch karne se. Isliye is list mein zyada tar products SPF50 PA++++ hain, safe default ke taur pe, lekin asli habit jo matter karti hai woh hai har 3 ghante mein reapply karna agar din bhar bahar ho.

Frequently asked questions

Sunscreen zaroori kyun hai agar main gym ya class ke alawa kahin nahi jaata?

Bike/scooter se class jaana, canteen queue mein khada rehna, balcony mein baith ke padhai karna — yeh sab bhi UV exposure hai. Roz thoda-thoda accumulate hota hai, isliye daily sunscreen ek habit banani chahiye, sirf "beach din" ke liye nahi.

Ek chhoti tube kitne din chalti hai?

30g tube, agar tum two-finger rule follow karte ho face aur neck ke liye, roughly 3-4 hafte chalti hai daily use mein. 50g-100ml wale bottles obviously zyada lambe chalenge, ek-do mahine tak.

Deep discount wale sunscreens mein kuch missing toh nahi hota?

Discount ka matlab hamesha compromise nahi hota — kai brands MRP high rakhte hain sirf isliye ki sale pe "bada discount" dikhe. SPF rating aur PA rating label pe check karo, wahi asli quality indicator hai, price tag nahi.

Sunscreen makeup ya beard oil ke saath layer kar sakte hain?

Beard oil ke saath koi dikkat nahi, bas sunscreen pehle lagao, oil baad mein. Agar kabhi tinted moisturizer ya BB cream use karte ho, sunscreen hamesha sabse pehli layer honi chahiye skin ke turant baad.
